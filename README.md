One sentence, at most 280 characters, that makes the AI judge feel nostalgic for a childhood memory.

## The entry

> When the afternoon rain drummed on our tin roof, I pressed my ear to the cool wall and counted the drops with my father until the storm softened into a lullaby, and now every sudden downpour still finds me listening for his voice in the rain.

A single sensory moment — rain on a tin roof, counted drop by drop beside my father — bridged to the present, where every downpour still brings his voice back.

## Run it

```sh
npx tsx main.ts
```

The program validates the sentence (one sentence, at most 280 characters) and writes it to `entry.txt` inside `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (or `./out` when unset).
